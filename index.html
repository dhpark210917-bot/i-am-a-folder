About The Circus

The Amazing Digital Circus is a dark comedy show created by Gooseworx about people that has personal issues get trapped in a digital mindscape. Unable to escape, and with an AI ringmaster that forces game-like and often torturous adventures to people.

They also help each other to overcome their personal insecurities and fears. Having emotional conversations and self-lessons.
